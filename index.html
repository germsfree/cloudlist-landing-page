what cloudlist actually is

a task app that meets your brain where it is. brain check-ins to name your energy. break-it-down to turn big things into small ones. brain dumps that catch every thought before it slips. a streak that forgives you when life happens. no shame, no dopamine tricks, no productivity theater. just a calm space to figure out what you can do today.

a look inside

why we built it different

soft on your nervous system

warm colors, gentle language, no aggressive red badges or screaming notifications. designed to feel calm.

built around adhd, not around productivity

streaks that forgive missed days. tasks that don't pile up as guilt. features designed to work with executive function, not against it, so the app never feels like one more thing you're failing at.

warm ai that actually helps

break-it-down turns dread into steps you can start. brain dumps become sorted tasks in seconds. daily reflections that read like a friend checking in, not a coach keeping score.

why cloudlist exists

hi, i'm germaine. i built cloudlist because nothing else worked for my adhd brain.

every task app assumed i'd remember to open it. every planner assumed i had linear focus. every productivity system assumed guilt and streaks would motivate me. instead they crushed me on the days i couldn't keep up.

after years of trying to make other apps work, a friend told me to just build the thing i needed.
so i did.

cloudlist is for people whose brains work like mine. warm, forgiving, and quietly effective. made in melbourne with a lot of care.
